# RUdown: a three-minute demo

1. Open RUdown. Lead with: **“We turn ‘anyone down?’ into a plan everyone can afford.”**
2. Choose **Explore a demo**. Show the four people, their budgets, and the activity cards.
3. Search **08901** in the map. Select the New Brunswick result. Explain that users can use a ZIP code instead of a precise address.
4. Open **Golden hour at the gardens**. Show the separate per-person and group costs, each person's estimated distance, and the $16 shares.
5. Select **Make this our plan**. Review the itinerary and each person's share.
6. Check the acknowledgment and approve your share. The demo shows **1 of 4 approved**. Other people's approvals are never fabricated.
7. Add **Parking — $10 — For the group**, shared by all four. Shares become **$18.50**, and the prior approval is cleared.
8. Show **Ideas & votes**. Vote for the picnic idea. Explain that voting does not authorize spending.
9. Show **Group wallet**. Connect a compatible browser wallet if available. The balance comes from Solana Devnet. Be explicit: **escrow deposits and refunds are not enabled yet**.

To show true shared persistence, create a real group and open its invite from a second browser profile or device that can access the same deployment. Each participant uses their own display name and only controls their own preferences, vote, and approval. Hosted private preview access is separate from group invitations.

Create separate groups with **Day trip** or **Overnight trip** to demonstrate those catalogs. Overnight plans include accommodation and food estimates. The seeded locations cover the New Brunswick area and regional getaways; searching another city moves the map but does not invent a live venue catalog.

## Honest demo notes

- Activities, prices, capacities, and itineraries are seeded examples, not verified venue listings.
- Photos are illustrative; they do not identify the actual venue.
- Map tiles and location search are live. Distances and carpool detours are straight-line estimates, not traffic-aware driving routes.
- Database-backed groups persist; the sample **Explore a demo** group resets on refresh.
- No venue is booked, no funds move, and test SOL is not real money.
