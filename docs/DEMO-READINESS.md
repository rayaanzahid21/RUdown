# Three-minute demo and honest status

1. Show the product immediately. Change three people's budgets and explain integer-cent cost sharing.
2. Search a real location; show ratings, photos and hours. Select a venue and a date.
3. Have a second device decline for budget or time. Rebuild the plan and show approvals reset.
4. Enter each person's route time and fare; approve the exact revised shares.
5. In Group wallet, show the recipient and test amount. Sign a Devnet contribution, verify it, and open Explorer. If the wallet/faucet is unavailable, say so; do not present a preview as a transaction.

Card check: use Stripe sandbox Checkout with 4242 4242 4242 4242, a future expiry and any three-digit CVC. A return page alone is not proof; refresh the Stripe-verified receipt.

Calendar check: authorize your Google account, sync an approved plan, open Calendar and verify title/time/location. Edit a cost; verify approvals clear and the calendar event becomes tentative. Reapprove and confirm the same event updates.

Recovery check: save your private code from The crew, open the public site on another device, restore membership from the home screen. Never show the private code to judges. Test the public home page in an unsigned browser and on cellular yourself.

Fallback: the editable sample planner and generated venue illustrations remain usable if individual external services fail after page load. Do not claim offline collaboration. Record a short successful walkthrough before presenting, and label any playback as a recording.

Technical explanation: costs use integer cents; shared leftovers follow stable member order. Budgets are never raised automatically. Approvals are per member, exact cents and plan version. Edits invalidate approvals. Solana verifies a test transfer with a memo tied to a unique member/plan intent; a plan approval is not an on-chain contract. No escrow/refund guarantee is made.
